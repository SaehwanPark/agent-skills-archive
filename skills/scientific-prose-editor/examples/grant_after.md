# Significance

Treatment resistance complicates decisions about initial therapy. This proposal addresses a specific gap: whether a baseline risk model remains well calibrated across clinical sites. Its contribution is to quantify risk and assess transportability in data already approved for secondary analysis.

# Specific Aims

Aim 1: Estimate baseline resistance risk among 600 adults in an existing observational cohort. Aim 2: Evaluate calibration of the model across three clinical sites. We will use data already approved for secondary analysis.
