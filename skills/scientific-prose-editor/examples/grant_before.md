# Significance

This groundbreaking proposal will revolutionize the way researchers study treatment resistance. In today's rapidly evolving biomedical landscape, treatment resistance plays a pivotal role in patient outcomes. A new model is therefore needed. The project is innovative, impactful, and transformative.

# Specific Aims

Aim 1: Estimate baseline resistance risk among 600 adults in an existing observational cohort. Aim 2: Evaluate calibration of the model across three clinical sites. We will use data already approved for secondary analysis.
