# Disclosure and originality

Check current rules for the intended journal, funder, or institution. Editing does not change the work's provenance. Keep confidential drafts within authorized tools and preserve the facts needed for an accurate disclosure.

- [ICMJE](https://www.icmje.org/recommendations/browse/artificial-intelligence/ai-use-by-authors.html) calls for disclosure of AI-assisted use at submission, in the cover letter and the appropriate part of the manuscript. Authors retain responsibility for accuracy, attribution, and originality.
- [NIH notice NOT-OD-25-132](https://grants.nih.gov/grants/guide/notice-files/NOT-OD-25-132.html) says NIH will not treat applications or sections substantially developed by AI as original applicant ideas. The notice allows for limited assistance in some circumstances. Later copyediting does not alter how the application was developed.
