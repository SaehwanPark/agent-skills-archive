# Provenance, disclosure and venue policy

The skill improves scientific prose but cannot alter who developed the underlying research ideas, arguments or text. Use in ways consistent with authors' and institutions' responsibilities.

- **ICMJE (updated January 2026):** authors should disclose AI-assisted technology in submitted work and cover letters, describing how it was used; humans are accountable for accuracy, originality and citations. See https://www.icmje.org/recommendations/browse/artificial-intelligence/ai-use-by-authors.html
- **NIH notice NOT-OD-25-132:** for applications with receipt dates September 25, 2025 onward, NIH will not consider applications or sections substantially developed by AI to be applicants' original ideas. Limited AI assistance can be appropriate. See https://grants.nih.gov/grants/guide/notice-files/NOT-OD-25-132.html
- Journal, funder, institution, and conference requirements vary. Where policies are unclear, check the current official guidance rather than assert blanket permission.
- Detector scores are not conclusive provenance evidence. See https://aclanthology.org/2026.emnlp-industry.109/ for large-scale empirical evidence on editing-related detector false positives.
- Pangram reports strong internal model performance, including mixed and AI-assisted segments, but performance estimates are domain- and protocol-dependent. Its current model-card limitations include text dominated by mathematics or templated technical material: https://www.pangram.com/research/model-card/pangram-4
- No stealth claims, ghost authorship stories, counterfeit revision histories or fabricated disclosures.
- Do not upload confidential manuscripts, unpublished grant materials or identifiable patient information to public detector APIs without appropriate authorization and agreement.

A compliant workflow retains an internal editing record: author-owned research plan, supplied source drafts, permitted tool usage, material changes and required disclosures. This is a recommendation for provenance integrity, not a method to game detection.
